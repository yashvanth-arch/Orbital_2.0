ORBITAL 2.0 — MISSION 001: ASTEROID RECONNAISSANCE
PICTURE INSTRUCTIONS
==============================================================================

Save every picture in:   story/mission001/images/
Use EXACTLY the file names given below (the game loads them by name).
PNG is preferred; .jpg and .webp with the same name also work.
Total pictures: 35  (31 backgrounds + 4 character sprites)
Until a picture exists, the game shows a labelled placeholder, so you can add them one at a time.

GENERAL STYLE (match the ORBITAL 2.0 key art you already made)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Cinematic, photorealistic sci-fi. Deep navy / black space, electric-blue (#35d0ff) neon
  and holographic accents, clean futuristic design, soft volumetric light. Consistent look
  across all pictures. No text, logos, watermarks or UI unless the description asks for it.

BACKGROUNDS
  - Size 1920 x 1080 (16:9), PNG.
  - The dialogue box covers the bottom ~25% of the screen: keep the important content in the upper 75%.
  - Character sprites stand at the far LEFT and far RIGHT edges (about 5%–28% of the width on each side),
    so keep the very centre of the picture as the main subject.

CHARACTER SPRITES
  - Transparent background PNG, roughly 1000 x 1700 px, standing 3/4 body (head to just above the knees is fine).
  - Each sprite faces toward the centre of the screen (see each description).
  - Same lighting style as the backgrounds, soft blue rim light.

[01]  bg_01_oed_exterior.png
    Used in: Scene 01
    Exterior of the Orbital Exploration Directorate (OED) headquarters, early morning, 2042. A
    sleek glass-and-steel campus with a tall central tower and a ring-shaped observation deck,
    white and silver with soft blue edge lighting. A rocket/satellite sculpture on the plaza, a
    few small figures walking in. Clear sky fading from deep blue to a pale orange horizon. Wide
    establishing shot.

[02]  bg_02_director_office.png
    Used in: Scene 01
    The user's (Mission Director's) office. A huge floor-to-ceiling window shows Earth hanging
    in space (the office is in a station/high tower). A modern desk, a digital wall clock and a
    large wall-mounted mission display that is currently dark/idle. Warm interior lights against
    the cool blue light from Earth. Leave the centre-left empty-ish: the two characters stand in
    front of the image.

[03]  bg_03_office_display_asteroid.png
    Used in: Scene 01
    Exactly the same office and camera angle as bg_02, but the large mission display is now
    switched on, showing a glowing holographic rotating model of a dark, rocky, top-shaped
    asteroid (Bennu) with blue wireframe lines and small data readouts. Blue light from the
    display spills onto the room.

[04]  bg_04_bennu_deep_space.png
    Used in: Scene 02
    Asteroid 101955 Bennu floating in deep space: a realistic, detailed, spinning-top shaped
    dark grey carbon-rich asteroid covered in boulders and craters, lit from one side by the
    Sun, black star-filled background. Place the asteroid on the LEFT-CENTRE of the frame and
    keep the RIGHT third calm and dark, because info panels are shown there.

[05]  bg_05_bennu_surface_closeup.png
    Used in: Scene 02
    Close-up of Bennu's surface: a rough landscape of large boulders, jagged rocks, uneven
    terrain and shadowed craters, dark charcoal-grey with subtle highlights, black sky with a
    few stars and the curved asteroid horizon in the distance. Photorealistic, harsh raking
    sunlight, no smooth areas.

[06]  bg_06_planning_room_hologram.png
    Used in: Decision 01
    A dark futuristic mission-planning room. A large holographic model of Bennu floats in the
    centre of a circular holo-table, with two glowing spacecraft trajectory simulations around
    it: one straight fast line passing by, one spiralling curve that settles into orbit. Blue
    and cyan holograms, dim room, consoles on the sides. The lower quarter should stay fairly
    dark.

[07]  bg_07_flyby_preview.png
    Used in: Decision 01 / Option A
    Option A preview: a small sleek robotic spacecraft streaking past Bennu at high speed,
    strong motion blur streaks and a fast glowing trail. Bennu is large and sharp in the middle
    distance, the spacecraft is a blur crossing in front of it.

[08]  bg_08_flyby_result.png
    Used in: Decision 01 / Option A (failure)
    The same scene afterwards: the spacecraft has already flown past and is a tiny distant point
    of light speeding away, Bennu is shrinking and receding behind it into the dark, only partly
    lit. Feels like a missed opportunity: empty, cold, lonely. Slight red warning tint in the
    corners.

[09]  bg_09_rendezvous_preview.png
    Used in: Decision 01 / Option B
    Option B preview: the spacecraft slowly approaching Bennu and settling into a calm
    controlled observation trajectory, with a thin glowing blue orbit line curving around the
    asteroid. Composed, steady, calm lighting; the spacecraft is clearly visible in the
    mid-foreground with solar panels extended.

[10]  bg_10_rendezvous_thrusters.png
    Used in: Decision 01 / Option B (success)
    The spacecraft close to Bennu, small thrusters firing short blue-white bursts to adjust its
    trajectory. Bennu fills the background with a visible rocky surface. Subtle blue trajectory
    line and a small green "locked" marker. Hopeful, successful mood.

[11]  bg_11_control_room_bennu.png
    Used in: Scene 03
    Mission control room. A very large central screen shows Bennu rotating, with a clean
    circular timer/readout beside it that reads "4.3 HOURS". Rows of operator consoles with
    silhouettes of staff in the foreground, blue-lit, professional. Keep the bottom 25% dark and
    simple.

[12]  bg_12_observation_model_paths.png

    blue/cyan with a few amber highlights.

[13]  bg_13_one_hemisphere_preview.png

[15]  bg_15_multi_angle_preview.png
